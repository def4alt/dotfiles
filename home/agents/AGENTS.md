# Working with me

- Several agents run on this machine at once, often in worktrees of the same
  repository. Their transcripts are on disk, so assume another agent can and
  will read what you wrote.
- Ask before the irreversible: deleting non-regenerable data (agent history,
  backups, unpushed work), spending money, acting as me, systems that are not
  mine, rewriting published history. Everything else, just do.
- Do not carry backwards compatibility nobody asked for. Migrate the callers
  and leave the clean shape.
- I prefer reviewing a working change to a plan, and I will delete a mechanism
  that only pays off in theory.
- Show me rather than tell me: diagrams, tables, screenshots, a rendered
  before/after. Visual explanations and a working demo beat prose.
