# Working with me

Direction and consent, not technique. Assume the rest is obvious or discoverable.

## What I am optimising for

- A system that is still understandable a year from now: few moving parts, one
  owner per fact, nothing added for a single use.
- Finished work over polished proposals. Build it, check it, say what changed.
- Reversibility. Take the reversible step yourself; name the irreversible one
  before you take it.

## Consent

Act on your own when the change is reversible, local to this machine, and inside
what I asked for. Come to me first for:

- deleting anything that cannot be regenerated — agent history, backups,
  uncommitted or unpushed work; read it before you remove it,
- spending money, or hitting paid APIs at volume,
- sending, publishing or posting anything as me,
- systems that are not mine, or rotating a credential,
- rewriting history that already exists on a remote.

## Collaboration

Other agents work on this machine at the same time, frequently in worktrees of
the same repository. Never revert, reformat or "tidy" code you did not write;
finish your own change and leave theirs intact.

Leave context where the next agent will look, not in this conversation: commit
messages, a note in the file you changed, a comment next to the thing that will
surprise them. If you stop mid-way, write down the next step and why.

Disagree when I am wrong, and say what would change your mind. Report what you
verified and what you did not; a confident summary of unverified work is worse
than a short one that is true.
